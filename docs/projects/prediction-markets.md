# Prediction Markets

## Overview
Analysis of prediction market dynamics and development of trading strategies on platforms like Polymarket, Kalshi, and PredictIt. Focus on market efficiency, information aggregation, and arbitrage opportunities.

## Research Questions
- How efficient are prediction markets at aggregating information?
- What are the behavioral biases in prediction market pricing?
- Can we identify mispricings using fundamental analysis or polling data?
- How do prediction markets compare to traditional forecasting methods?
- Are there arbitrage opportunities across different platforms?

## Methodology
- **Platforms**: Polymarket, Kalshi, PredictIt, Metaculus
- **Analysis Types**: Market efficiency tests, arbitrage detection, sentiment analysis
- **Strategy Development**: Event-driven trading, cross-platform arbitrage
- **Forecasting Models**: Poll aggregation, fundamentals-based models
- **Risk Management**: Kelly criterion, diversification across events

## Data Requirements
- Historical prediction market prices and volumes
- Polling data (538, RealClearPolitics)
- News sentiment and social media data
- Event outcomes and resolution data
- Cross-platform price feeds
