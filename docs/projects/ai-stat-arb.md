# Statistical Arbitrage of AI Supply Chain

## Overview
Investigating statistical arbitrage opportunities across AI hardware and software companies, focusing on mean-reversion strategies and pair trading within the AI supply chain ecosystem.

## Research Questions
- What are the cointegration relationships between AI chip manufacturers and cloud providers?
- How do AI model releases impact correlated stock movements?
- Can earnings announcements from key players predict moves in related stocks?
- What are optimal holding periods for mean-reversion strategies in this sector?

## Methodology
- **Universe Selection**: NVIDIA, AMD, TSMC, Microsoft, Google, AWS, etc.
- **Statistical Tests**: Cointegration (Johansen test), correlation analysis
- **Strategy Types**: Pairs trading, basket trading, factor models
- **Execution**: Market-neutral, beta-hedged portfolios
- **Performance Metrics**: Sharpe ratio, maximum drawdown, turnover

## Data Requirements
- Daily/intraday stock prices for AI supply chain companies
- Earnings announcement dates and estimates
- Product release timelines (GPUs, AI models)
- Supply chain relationship mappings
- Sentiment data from news and social media
