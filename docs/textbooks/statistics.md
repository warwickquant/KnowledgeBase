# Statistics Textbooks

Statistical methods and machine learning for quantitative finance.

---

## Statistical Inference

### Statistical Inference
**Authors**: George Casella and Roger L. Berger  
**Publisher**: Duxbury Press, 2002  
**Link**: [Cengage](https://www.cengage.com/c/statistical-inference-2e-casella-berger/9780534243128/)

**Description**: Comprehensive graduate-level text covering probability theory, statistical inference, and hypothesis testing. Fundamental for understanding parameter estimation, confidence intervals, and statistical tests used in backtesting and model validation. Essential foundation for quantitative research.

---

## Time Series Analysis

### Analysis of Financial Time Series (3rd Edition)
**Author**: Ruey S. Tsay  
**Publisher**: Wiley, 2010  
**Link**: [Wiley](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Analysis+of+Financial+Time+Series%2C+3rd+Edition-p-9780470414354)

**Description**: Authoritative text on financial time series modeling covering ARMA, GARCH, volatility models, and high-frequency data analysis. Includes R code implementations. Critical for forecasting returns, modeling volatility clustering, and understanding autocorrelation in financial data. Direct applications to systematic trading strategies.

---

## Machine Learning

### An Introduction to Statistical Learning: with Applications in R
**Authors**: James, G., Witten, D., Hastie, T., & Tibshirani, R.  
**Publisher**: Springer, 2013  
**Link**: [Springer](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4614-7138-7)

**Description**: Accessible introduction to modern statistical learning methods including regression, classification, tree-based methods, support vector machines, and neural networks. Balances theory with practical R implementations. Essential for applying machine learning to trading signal generation, factor discovery, and portfolio construction. Excellent for beginners.

**Note**: Free PDF available from authors' website. Python implementations available via `scikit-learn`.

---

## Computational Statistics

### Bayesian Data Analysis
**Authors**: Andrew Gelman, John B. Carlin, Hal S. Stern, David B. Dunson, Aki Vehtari, and Donald B. Rubin  
**Publisher**: CRC Press, 2013 (3rd Edition)  
**Link**: [CRC Press](https://www.taylorfrancis.com/books/mono/10.1201/b16018/bayesian-data-analysis-andrew-gelman-john-carlin-hal-stern-david-dunson-aki-vehtari-donald-rubin)

**Description**: Comprehensive treatment of Bayesian statistical methods covering prior selection, MCMC sampling, hierarchical models, and model checking. Applications to parameter estimation under uncertainty, probabilistic forecasting, and adaptive trading strategies. Critical for quantitative researchers working with small datasets or requiring uncertainty quantification.

### Monte Carlo Statistical Methods
**Authors**: Christian P. Robert and George Casella  
**Publisher**: Springer, 2004 (2nd Edition)  
**Link**: [Springer](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-4757-4145-2)

**Description**: Techniques for simulation-based statistical inference and numerical integration. Essential for options pricing, risk estimation (VaR, CVaR), and backtesting strategies under various market scenarios. Covers variance reduction, importance sampling, and sequential Monte Carlo methods.
