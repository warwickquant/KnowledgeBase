# Finance Textbooks

Core quantitative finance texts covering options, portfolio management, and statistical arbitrage.

---

## Options Trading & Volatility

### Option Volatility and Pricing: Advanced Trading Strategies and Techniques
**Author**: Sheldon Natenberg  
**Publisher**: McGraw-Hill, 1994  
**Link**: [McGraw-Hill](https://www.mhprofessional.com/9781557384867-usa-option-volatility-and-pricing-advanced-trading-strategies-and-techniques)

**Description**: Industry-standard text for understanding options pricing, Greeks, and volatility trading. Covers Black-Scholes, implied volatility, volatility surfaces, and practical trading strategies. Essential for market-makers and vol traders. Intuitive explanations with focus on practical application rather than heavy mathematics. Must-read for the Systematic Crypto Options Trading project.

**Key Topics**: Delta, gamma, theta, vega hedging; volatility smile; calendar spreads; risk management

---

## Portfolio Management

### Advanced Portfolio Management: A Quant's Guide for Fundamental Investors
**Author**: Giuseppe A. Paleologo  
**Publisher**: Wiley, 2021  
**Link**: [Wiley](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Advanced+Portfolio+Management%3A+A+Quant%27s+Guide+for+Fundamental+Investors-p-9781119757252)

**Description**: Modern portfolio construction techniques bridging quantitative and fundamental investing. Covers risk models, factor analysis, alpha generation, and portfolio optimization with practical constraints. Written by author of `@__paleologo`. Excellent for understanding how quant techniques apply to active management. Relevant for statistical arbitrage and systematic strategies.

**Key Topics**: Risk decomposition, factor models, transaction costs, portfolio construction algorithms

---

## Statistical Arbitrage

### Quantitative Portfolio Management: The Art and Science of Statistical Arbitrage
**Author**: Michael Isichenko  
**Publisher**: Wiley, 2021  
**Link**: [Wiley](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Quantitative+Portfolio+Management%3A+The+Art+and+Science+of+Statistical+Arbitrage-p-9781119800071)

**Description**: Comprehensive guide to statistical arbitrage covering pairs trading, mean-reversion strategies, and market-neutral portfolio construction. Detailed treatment of cointegration, factor models, and execution strategies. Includes real-world implementation challenges like transaction costs, slippage, and capacity constraints. Direct application to AI Supply Chain Statistical Arbitrage project.

**Key Topics**: Pairs trading, basket trading, market neutrality, alpha decay, optimal execution

## Additional Recommended Topics

### Derivatives Pricing
- *Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives* by John Hull - Standard reference for derivatives
- *The Concepts and Practice of Mathematical Finance* by Mark Joshi - Rigorous derivatives pricing

### Risk Management
- *Quantitative Risk Management* by McNeil, Frey, and Embrechts - VaR, CVaR, extreme value theory
- *Active Portfolio Management* by Grinold and Kahn - Information ratio, fundamental law of active management

### Market Microstructure
- *Trading and Exchanges* by Larry Harris - Market structure and liquidity
- *Algorithmic and High-Frequency Trading* by Cartea, Jaimungal, and Penalva - Order execution, market impact
