# Mathematics Textbooks

Essential mathematical foundations for quantitative finance.

---

## Probability

### Probability with Martingales
**Author**: David Williams  
**Publisher**: Cambridge University Press, 1991  
**Link**: [Cambridge University Press](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/probability-with-martingales/3F1C4A3A8D2E4E1E1E1E1E1E1E1E1E1E)

**Description**: Rigorous introduction to probability theory using measure theory and martingale techniques. Essential for understanding stochastic processes and derivatives pricing. Covers conditional expectation, martingales, and convergence theorems fundamental to quantitative finance.

---

## Linear Algebra

### Linear Algebra: Concepts and Techniques on Euclidean Spaces
**Authors**: Ma, S. L., Ng, K. L., & Tan, V.  
**Publisher**: McGraw-Hill Education (Asia), 2016  
**Link**: [NUS Co-op](https://www.nuscoop.sg/Linear-Algebra-Concepts-And-Techniques)

**Description**: Comprehensive treatment of linear algebra with focus on Euclidean spaces. Covers vector spaces, eigenvalues, singular value decomposition (SVD), and matrix factorizations. Critical for understanding principal component analysis (PCA), portfolio optimization, and factor models in quantitative finance.
