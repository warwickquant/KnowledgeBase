# Research Paper Summaries

Curated summaries of important papers in quantitative finance. Members are encouraged to contribute summaries of papers they've read.

---

## How to Use This Section

- **Find papers** relevant to your projects or interests
- **Read summaries** to decide if full paper is worth your time
- **Contribute summaries** of papers you've read (see [Community Contributions](./README.md))

---

## Template for Contributions

When contributing a paper summary, use this format:

```markdown
### [Paper Title]
**Authors**: [Names]  
**Year**: [Publication year]  
**Journal/Source**: [Where published]  
**Link**: [URL if available publicly]

**Summary**:
[2-3 paragraphs explaining: What question does the paper address? What methodology do they use? What are the key findings?]

**Key Takeaways**:
- [Bullet point 1]
- [Bullet point 2]
- [Bullet point 3]

**Relevance to Projects**:
[How this paper relates to society projects or interests]

**Implementation Notes**:
[Any practical insights on implementing ideas from the paper]

**Limitations**:
[Weaknesses or caveats to be aware of]

**Further Reading**:
[Related papers or resources]
```

## Contributing

Have you read a great paper? Share your summary!

1. Use the template above

## Resources for Finding Papers

### Open Access
- [SSRN Quantitative Finance](https://www.ssrn.com/index.cfm/en/janda/janda-qf/)
- [arXiv q-fin](https://arxiv.org/list/q-fin/recent)
- [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/)

### University Access
- Journal of Finance
- Journal of Financial Economics
- Review of Financial Studies
- Journal of Portfolio Management
- Quantitative Finance

### Practitioner Publications
- [Oxford-Man Institute](https://oxford-man.ox.ac.uk/research/)
- Trading firm research (Citadel, AQR, Two Sigma publish some)
- Central bank working papers (Fed, ECB)
