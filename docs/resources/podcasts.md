## Podcasts

1. **[Flirting with Models](https://www.flirtingwithmodels.com/)** - Corey Hoffstein (Newfound Research): Systematic investing, portfolio construction, quantitative strategies, factor investing, diversification, implementation details

2. **[Top Traders Unplugged](https://open.spotify.com/show/1ETt1dCpYajVhDSFNAiQlP)** - Niels Kaastrup-Larsen: Systematic trading, trend following, managed futures, CTA interviews, strategy development, risk management

3. **[Bloomberg Odd Lots](https://www.bloomberg.com/oddlots)** - Joe Weisenthal and Tracy Alloway: Markets, economics, market structure, economic trends, timely market commentary

4. **[Acquired](https://www.acquired.fm/)** - Ben Gilbert and David Rosenthal: Company histories, business strategy, tech company analysis

5. **[Dwarkesh Podcast](https://www.dwarkesh.com/)** - Dwarkesh Patel: Technology, AI, science, AI trends and sector dynamics
